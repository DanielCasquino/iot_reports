\subsection{Calibration and Validation}
The proposed enclosure uses a detachable support to keep the camera, color sensor, and servo aligned with the panel. Commercial button pushers such as SwitchBot Bot inform the external press-and-release mechanism~\cite{switchbot}; their compatibility is not assumed for this heater. The support must allow removal for manual access. Its attachment, dimensions, and servo travel will be selected from the panel geometry and button tests. The detachable panel module is distinct from the flow meter, whose installation requires opening and resealing an external plumbing union. The proposed YF-S201 has nominal male G1/2 connections~\cite{seeedflow}; the size and thread type of the heater's flexible connection have not been measured. Before procurement, mating fittings, available space, pressure and temperature ratings, and a location clear of the relief-valve discharge must be verified. An adapter or a different meter may be required.

The camera is aligned and shielded against glare. Recognized digits are compared with the visible panel over several temperatures; blank images and heater error codes are marked unavailable. The color classifier is checked under red, green, dark, and ambient-light conditions. The flow meter is calibrated with a measured water volume; pulses per liter and the installation point are recorded. Finally, the servo stroke and return are tested on the momentary button, and on/off requests are compared with the observed panel response. The electronics use a separate low-voltage supply inside a removable case. The case design leaves the heater's ventilation, safety discharge, and ordinary access unobstructed. The external water union must be checked for fitting size, rated pressure and temperature, leaks, and its position relative to the relief valve before installation.
