The proposed system targets residential users who own conventional electric storage water heaters
without integrated smart connectivity, particularly households seeking remote monitoring and control
without replacing an existing appliance. Its primary value proposition is the modernization of
conventional water heaters through an external, non-invasive IoT retrofit.
By integrating optical temperature acquisition, water consumption monitoring, mechanical actuation,
and a web-based interface, the system provides remote access to information and controls that would otherwise
require direct interaction with the appliance.
The solution is initially designed for the Rheem EW1-50RP, while its modular architecture offers the possibility of
adaptation to other water heater models with similar physical interfaces.

A potential business model consists of commercializing the solution as a retrofit kit that includes the embedded controller,
sensing modules, mechanical actuators, protective enclosure, and access to the monitoring platform.
Revenue could be generated through direct kit sales and an optional installation and configuration service,
with future opportunities for model-specific adaptations and technical maintenance.
This approach positions the product as an alternative to purchasing a new smart water heater,
emphasizing the reuse of existing appliances and the addition of connected functionality.
