This paper proposes a removable Internet of Things (IoT) retrofit for remote monitoring and on/off control of a
Rheem EW1-50RP electric storage water heater without accessing its internal electronics or switching its mains supply.
An ESP32-CAM captures the existing temperature display for application-side optical recognition, a TCS34725 color sensor
observes the heating indicator, and a YF-S201 Hall-effect sensor measures flow and accumulated water volume at an external
pipe connection. An ESP32 controller communicates over Wi-Fi/MQTT and operates a low-voltage servo that presses the original
on/off button following validated requests. The application displays observed states and supports basic voice commands while
identifying uncertain or unavailable readings. The proposed validation compares optical readings with the panel, calibrates
flow against measured volume, and verifies button response and mechanical compatibility. This is a prototype design; measured
performance and energy savings are not claimed.
