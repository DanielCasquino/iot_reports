\subsection{Problem Description and Justification}

For the selected Rheem heater, the user must approach the panel to read the tank-center temperature or press its on/off button~\cite{rheemmanual}. This limits remote preparation of hot water and makes it difficult to check the heater's observed state when away from the appliance. The original proposal also considered electrical and water consumption. The revised prototype retains water-use measurement, but it does not infer electrical energy from a light indicator or a nominal power rating.

Research on domestic water heating shows that scheduling and control can reduce energy use under studied conditions~\cite{booysen2019}; those results do not establish savings for this heater or this prototype. The immediate, testable problem is remote visibility and control without opening the heater or switching its mains supply. Flow data add a record of when and how much water passes the selected external point, which can help distinguish heating from actual water use~\cite{lee2021,pirow2018}.
