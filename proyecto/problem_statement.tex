\subsection{Problem Description}

Electric water heaters generally operate under manual control only: users must physically turn these appliances on/off. This interaction dependency introduces two main problems:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Users may forget to turn the water heater off
    \item Users might not be present when they need hot water, requiring to turn the heater on in advance
\end{itemize}

Both of these issues increase energy consumption without direct benefits, increasing costs and environmental impact~\cite{bhati2017}.

Lack of real-time monitoring and feedback is also a relevant problem. Users cannot easily check the current temperature of the water or the power status of the heater without being physically present.

Meanwhile, the heater's electric configuration introduces a different set of implementation problems. For instance, the activation of submerged high-power resistors requires proper insulation. A robust IoT system that can handle these requirements must guarantee the separation between low and high voltage circuits.
