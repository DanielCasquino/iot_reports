\IEEEPARstart{I}{nternet} of Things has transformed the way in which everyday appliances measure, communicate, and act in their environment. In a residential context, this technology enables \textit{smart homes}, which analyze and measure physical variables, automate decisions, and provide useful real-time data~\cite{alfuqaha2015}.

Within this ecosystem, home energy management has risen as a relevant field that aims to reduce energy consumption and decrease environmental impact~\cite{ford2017,zipperer2013}. In this context, electric water heaters stand out as one of the most energy-consuming household appliances, as they transform electric energy into heat by (most commonly) using $1.5-3kW$ resistors. These devices often require manual interaction, and as such, are prone to being left in a powered state for prolongued periods of time, wasting large amounts of energy.

For this reason, this document presents an IoT system proposal that aims to expand control capabilities over these devices, using sensors and microcontrollers to enable features such as remote control, temperature monitoring, and automated scheduling.\@
