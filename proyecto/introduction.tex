\IEEEPARstart{E}{lectric} water heaters are common household appliances that provide a continuous flow of hot water by utilizing heating elements. As heating consumes a moderate amout of energy, these devices have been progressively improved throughout the years to increase enery efficiency and reduce usage costs.

However, most available water heaters are manually activated/deactivated, and as such, require human interaction. For this reason, this document presents an IoT system proposal that aims to expand control capabilities over these devices, using sensors and microcontrollers to enable remote control, temperature monitoring, and automated scheduling.\@
