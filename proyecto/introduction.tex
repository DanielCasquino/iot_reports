\IEEEPARstart{D}{omestic} electric storage water heaters may require users to be near the appliance to read its temperature and operate its controls. The Rheem EW1-50RP (50 L, 1500 W) is the reference case for this project. It already measures and displays the temperature at the center of its tank, controls heating through its own thermostat, and offers an AI Smart mode~\cite{rheemmanual}. The missing function addressed here is remote access to the existing panel information and on/off button.

This proposal adds a removable external IoT layer. A camera reads the displayed temperature, an RGB sensor observes the heating pilot, and a flow sensor measures water use at an accessible pipe connection. A servo presses the original on/off button. An application presents the three measurements and sends an on/off request; a simple voice interface uses the same request path. The heater retains its own temperature setting and protections. The prototype is specific to the EW1-50RP, while the underlying access problem can affect other appliances with local-only controls.
